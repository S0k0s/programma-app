# App Review notes (paste into App Store Connect → App Review Information → Notes)

AI Coach is a personal fitness and nutrition app. Users answer a short
onboarding conversation and get a training program and daily calorie/macro
targets; they log workouts and meals and can chat with an AI coach.

**Sign-in:** email + password only (no third-party login, so Sign in with Apple
is not required). Age gate: users confirm they are 18+.

**Subscription (Guideline 3.1.2):** "AI Coach Pro" is sold only through
In-App Purchase as two auto-renewable subscriptions in one group: Monthly
(1 month, EUR 4.99) and Annual (1 year, EUR 39.99). The Pro screen (Profile tab)
shows each plan's price, the auto-renewal terms, Restore Purchases, and links to
the Terms of Use and Privacy Policy. Pro unlocks the AI Coach chat (beyond the
free onboarding conversation) and photo food scanning.
**Pro for review:** purchase with a Sandbox tester.

**Camera (Guideline 5.1.1):** used only when the user taps "Scan food with a
photo" on the Extra food screen (Pro), to capture a meal photo. The image is
sent to our server and an AI model to estimate calories/macros, then discarded
— it is not stored. The user reviews the estimate before saving.

**AI content (Guideline 1.4.1 / health):** advice is general and generated by
an AI; an in-app medical disclaimer is shown, and the Terms state it does not
replace professional advice.

**Account deletion (Guideline 5.1.1(v)):** Profile → Delete account (type
DELETE to confirm; also removes the user's stored data).

**Web content:** the app bundles its own files; it does not load a remote
website. It uses native haptics, local notifications (rest timer), in-app
purchase and the camera in addition to the app UI.
